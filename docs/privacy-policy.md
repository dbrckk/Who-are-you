# Who Are You? — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-05

Who Are You? is an entertainment and self-reflection app. Its quizzes are not medical or psychological diagnoses.

## Data stored on the device

The app stores quiz completion, latest quiz scores, Daily Question state, streaks, onboarding completion and the lifetime remove-ads entitlement locally on the device using Android DataStore.

## Advertising and consent

The free version can display Google AdMob interstitial advertising outside active quizzes. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used to request consent where required and ad requests are gated by the consent state returned by UMP.

Google and its advertising partners may process device, advertising and interaction data according to their own policies and the consent choices available to the user.

## Purchases

The optional lifetime remove-ads purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. The app does not directly collect or store payment card information. Purchase status may be queried from Google Play to restore the entitlement.

## Optional product telemetry

Production builds can be configured to send limited product events and error reports to a developer-controlled HTTPS telemetry endpoint. The current telemetry design does not require an account and is intended to avoid directly identifying users. The production operator must ensure the configured endpoint, retention period and legal basis match this policy before release.

Fatal crash reports may be stored briefly on-device and sent on the next launch when production telemetry is configured.

## Sharing

When a user chooses to share a result or challenge, Android's system share sheet is used. Shared content may include a generated result image, quiz/result text and a challenge link containing the quiz identifier and score. The app does not automatically publish this content.

## Accounts

No Who Are You? account is required for the current app experience.

## Children

The app is not specifically designed for children. The Play Console target-audience declaration must match the final distribution decision before publication.

## Data deletion

Because profile data is primarily stored locally, uninstalling the app removes that local app data according to Android behavior. If production telemetry is enabled, the operator must provide an appropriate contact/deletion path for any server-side records retained by that service.

## Third-party services

The app may use Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads / AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform. Their processing is governed by the applicable Google terms and privacy documentation.
