Who Are You? — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Who Are You? is an entertainment and self-reflection application. Its quizzes are not medical or psychological diagnoses. No Who Are You? account is required.

Santé Connect : accès facultatif aux pas et objectifs de bien-être

Dans Mes habitudes → Activité, Who Are You? demande uniquement l'autorisation android.permission.health.READ_STEPS pour lire le nombre de pas depuis Santé Connect, après votre accord explicite. L'application ne demande pas l'accès au sommeil, au rythme cardiaque ou aux dossiers médicaux et n'écrit aucune donnée dans Santé Connect. Les questionnaires restent utilisables sans cette autorisation.

Finalité : afficher les pas du jour, les moyennes et les tendances d'activité sur 7 et 30 jours ainsi que la progression des objectifs personnels de marche. Ces informations servent uniquement au suivi facultatif de l'activité physique et ne sont pas utilisées pour établir un diagnostic médical ou psychologique.

Accès, collecte et conservation : la lecture se fait sur votre appareil. Les totaux journaliers agrégés et les observations calculées sont enregistrés localement dans Android DataStore, sans synchronisation avec un serveur Who Are You?. Lorsque l'application fonctionne, l'historique couvre le jour en cours et les 30 jours précédents. Les anciennes observations sont supprimées à la prochaine ouverture ou actualisation ; si vous n'ouvrez plus l'application, elles peuvent donc rester localement au-delà de cette période. Les paramètres d'objectifs sont conservés jusqu'à leur suppression par l'utilisateur.

Partage et publicité : les pas et les objectifs ne sont ni vendus ni envoyés à nos serveurs, aux annonceurs ou à des outils d'analyse externes. Ils ne servent jamais au ciblage publicitaire. Le SDK publicitaire peut traiter séparément des données relatives à l'appareil et aux annonces, comme expliqué dans la section anglaise « Advertising and consent » ci-dessous.

Contrôle et suppression : vous pouvez refuser l'accès à Santé Connect. Dans Mes habitudes → Activité → Désactiver, vous pouvez supprimer les observations locales de pas ; Mes habitudes → Supprimer les données d'habitudes efface aussi les autres observations et objectifs. Pour empêcher de nouvelles lectures, révoquez séparément l'autorisation dans Paramètres Android → Santé Connect → Autorisations des applications → Who Are You? (le chemin exact dépend de l'appareil). Révoquer l'autorisation seule ne supprime pas les observations déjà enregistrées. Effacer les données de l'application ou la désinstaller supprime également les données locales.

Data stored on your device

The app stores quiz completion, latest and previous quiz scores, Daily Question state, streaks, onboarding completion, social comparison state and the lifetime remove-ads entitlement locally on the device using Android DataStore.

Health Connect: optional step tracking and wellness goals

The optional My Habits → Activity feature requests android.permission.health.READ_STEPS and reads step counts only from Android Health Connect, after you explicitly grant permission. It does not write steps or request heart rate, sleep, medical records or other Health Connect data. You do not need Health Connect for the personality quizzes.

Purpose: The app aggregates your step counts on the device to show daily step totals, recent 7/30-day activity averages and trends, and progress towards optional personal step-count goals. This is a fitness/wellness tracking feature and not a medical or psychological diagnosis.

Access, collection and storage: The step totals are read directly from Health Connect on your device and the aggregated daily totals and activity observations are saved locally in Android DataStore. When you use the app, it keeps a rolling window of observations for the current day and up to the preceding 30 days. Older records are cleared on the next app launch/refresh; if the app is not opened for a long time, old local records can remain until the app runs again or you delete them manually. Goal settings remain locally until you remove them or clear app data.

Sharing: Who Are You? does not upload, sell or share Health Connect steps or step goals with its servers, advertising providers, custom analytics, or other third parties. Health Connect step data is never used for advertising. Advertising services may separately process unrelated advertising/device information as described below.

Choice and deletion: You can refuse the permission without losing access to quizzes. In My Habits → Activity → Disable, you can erase the app's local step history and disable the feature. My Habits → Delete Habits Data removes all locally stored habits observations and goals. To revoke the Android permission as well, go to Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → Who Are You? (device paths may vary). Revoking the permission prevents further reads but does not automatically delete stored local step history. You may also clear the app's data or uninstall it. Step data is not stored on Who Are You? servers, so there is no remote step-data copy to request deletion of.

Optional app-usage observations

The separate optional My Habits → App usage feature requests Android Usage Access to display local screen-time summaries, app-use timing patterns and related goals. It processes app identifiers and time spent, not the contents of other apps. Observations remain locally on the device for the current day and up to the preceding 30 days. Disabling the source deletes its local observations; the Android permission must be revoked separately in device settings.

Advertising and consent

The free version can display Google AdMob interstitial advertising outside active quizzes. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used to request and manage consent where required. Advertising requests are gated by the consent state returned by UMP, and the app exposes privacy options when UMP reports that they must be available.

Google's Android disclosure guidance for the Google Mobile Ads SDK states that the SDK can automatically collect and share data such as IP address, product interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for purposes including advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Android advertising ID can be used when available and not disabled by the application or user/device settings.

Purchases

The optional lifetime remove-ads purchase is processed by Google Play Billing using product ID remove_ads_lifetime. The app does not directly collect or store payment-card information. Purchase status can be queried from Google Play to grant or restore the entitlement. Purchase tokens are not included in the app's custom analytics events.

Optional product telemetry

Production builds can be configured to send limited product events and minimized error reports to a developer-controlled HTTPS endpoint. If no production telemetry endpoint is configured, this custom HTTP telemetry is disabled.

When enabled, custom telemetry can include bounded app/screen events, quiz activity, sharing/challenge actions, retention events, ad impressions and premium purchase-funnel events. The custom analytics contract does not include names, email addresses, free-form user text, advertising IDs, purchase tokens or deep-link query parameters. Behavioral scores are converted to coarse ranges before analytics transmission.

Remote error telemetry excludes exception messages and stack traces. Fatal crash details may be buffered briefly on-device while a next-launch remote fatal-crash event is minimized.

Sharing and challenge links

Sharing is initiated by the user through Android's system share sheet. Shared content can include generated result/profile images, result text, compatibility information or a challenge link. Challenge URLs can encode the quiz identifier and challenge score so another device can complete the comparison. The app does not automatically publish shared content to a Who Are You? account or social network.

Children

The app is not specifically designed for children. The final target-audience and age-range declaration in Google Play Console must match the production advertising and consent configuration.

Data deletion

Most Who Are You? profile data is stored locally. The Profile screen includes a confirmed Reset profile data action that removes quiz progress, scores, streaks, comparisons and onboarding state from the device. Local application data can also be removed using Android's app-data controls or by uninstalling the app. If custom production telemetry is enabled and retained server-side, the production support contact will provide the applicable deletion/request process.

Third-party services

The app can use Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads / AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Those services are governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy documentation.
