# Who Are You? 0.1.0 — internal testing

## English

Discover your personality in fast visual tests, build an evolving profile and compare results with friends.

This internal-test build includes:
- 15 personality/self-reflection tests.
- English and French content.
- Persistent global profile and progress.
- Shareable result and profile cards.
- Friend challenge links and compatibility scores.
- Daily Question, streaks and achievements.
- Optional lifetime ad removal through Google Play Billing.
- Consent-aware AdMob integration.
- Optional privacy-conscious telemetry configuration.

For entertainment and self-reflection only; not a psychological diagnosis.

- Contenu intégral en français et en anglais.
